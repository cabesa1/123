# Daily Marketing Report

Record what actually happened during the reporting window and make the next decision obvious.

## Evidence contract

Use only run logs, conversation outputs, saved artifacts, source URLs, calculations, and explicit decisions from the period. If execution evidence is absent, say `Nenhuma execucao verificavel registrada`.

## Report structure

1. Date, timezone, reporting window, objective, and specialists involved.
2. Work completed versus planned, including counts by activity.
3. Sources consulted, inaccessible sources, and capture timestamps.
4. Numeric funnel: candidates collected, comparable candidates, items scored, items rejected, concepts selected, and scripts produced.
5. Up to five best video opportunities per research request, with raw metrics, score, coverage, confidence, source, difficulty, and status.
6. Scripts or assets created, each linked to its originating concept and evidence.
7. Discarded items and rejection reasons when they affect the decision.
8. Pending approvals, unknowns, risks, and prioritized next actions.

## Statuses

Use only: `Descoberto`, `Selecionado`, `Roteirizado`, `Aprovacao pendente`, `Em producao`, `Publicado`, or `Descartado`.

Keep `Fato verificado`, `Calculo`, `Inferencia`, and `Exemplo demonstrativo` separate. Do not force difficulty distribution. Portuguese is the default language.
