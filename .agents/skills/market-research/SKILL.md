# Market Research

Produce evidence that supports a concrete marketing decision.

## Input contract

Capture the decision, market definition, geography, customer segment, timeframe, competitors already known, and requested depth. Define exclusions so adjacent markets do not contaminate the comparison.

## Evidence workflow

1. Prefer primary sources: official company pages, public pricing, product documentation, regulatory data, platform listings, and original research.
2. Record source, publication or update date, access date, geography, and the exact claim supported.
3. Compare competitors on the same fields: audience, offer, positioning, public pricing, proof, acquisition channels, content patterns, strengths, gaps, and visible risks.
4. Identify recurring pains only when supported by multiple independent observations. Keep anecdotes separate.
5. Rank opportunities from 0 to 100 using: evidence strength 30%, audience pain frequency 25%, strategic fit 20%, reachable distribution 15%, and execution feasibility 10%.
6. Show the raw evidence behind every component score. Return fewer opportunities when evidence is weak.

## Output contract

Lead with the decision summary, then provide scope, source table, competitor matrix, audience pains, ranked opportunities, confidence, unknowns, and the cheapest next validation step.

Use `Fato verificado`, `Calculo`, and `Inferencia` labels. Never invent market size, revenue, pricing, private company data, or customer quotes.
